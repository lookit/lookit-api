jsPsych Options

Studies created with jsPsych rely on an underlying "core" package and plugins/extensions that must be loaded in order for your study to work properly. CHS always loads the core jsPsych package, along with the most commonly-used plugins/extensions.

The expandable sections below show you (1) the core package and plugins/extensions that are always available, and (2) a list of plugins/extensions that you can choose to add, if you need them for your study. In general you want to minimize how many plugins/extensions you include, because they will increase your experiment's loading time.

If you aren't sure yet which ones you'll need, leave these options alone for now.

If you have a specific plugin/extension you want to use that you don't see listed below, please contact us on Slack or at support@childrenhelpingscience.org. We can probably add it for you!

Your study automatically has access to the core jsPsych library and CHS-jsPsych packages listed below.
